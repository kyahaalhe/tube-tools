Privacy Policy

Short and plain. Last updated: November 2026.

What TubeTools does

TubeTools is a free toolkit for YouTube creators. It is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by YouTube or Google.

YouTube API Services

TubeTools uses YouTube API Services to read public video and channel information. By using TubeTools you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What we receive

  • Links and search terms. When you analyze a video or search, the link or text is sent to our server, which asks YouTube for public data. Results may be cached for a short time to save quota.
  • Saved history. Your recent analyses, action-plan ticks and daily search counters are stored only in your own browser (local storage). You can clear them in the analyzer or in your browser settings.
  • Feedback. If you send feedback through Tubi, we receive the rating, your message, the page you were on and any contact detail you chose to type. It goes to the site owner only. Please do not include passwords or private data.
  • Visit statistics. We use Vercel Web Analytics for anonymous visit statistics such as pages viewed, country and device type. We do not use advertising trackers.

What we do not do

We do not require accounts, sell your data, or ask for your YouTube login. Tools only read public information.

Your choices

You can clear saved history from your browser at any time. If you sent feedback with a contact detail and want it deleted, tell us through the feedback helper and mention the date.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change too.
