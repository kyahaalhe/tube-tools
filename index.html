Questions

Quick answers.

What is TubeTools?

A free toolkit for YouTube creators: video analysis, viral keywords, title and description checks, hashtags, thumbnails and SEO. It is an independent project and is not affiliated with YouTube or Google.

Is the viral score official?

No. It is based on public numbers (views per day, comparison with the channel's recent uploads, reach compared with subscribers, engagement, title and metadata) and rule-of-thumb benchmarks. Click-through rate and retention only exist in YouTube Studio.

Do I need an account?

No sign-up is needed. Your recent analyses are saved only in your own browser.

Why are searches limited?

YouTube gives a limited free quota per day, and a search costs far more than a video analysis. So repeated searches are saved, and each browser has a daily limit for searches and niche deep dives.

How do viral keywords work?

They are mined from trending videos or from recent, relevant videos for your topic. They show what is popular now, not search volume or competition.

Where does my feedback go?

To the site owner only, through Tubi, the little helper in the corner. See the Privacy Policy for details.
